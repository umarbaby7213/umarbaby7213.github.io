How to play

Four things and you know the whole game.

Tap the sky

Every tap on the dark airspace pays one gem. Taps on planes and buttons do their own job instead.

Buy a plane

Planes fly the loop on their own and pay gems every second whether you tap or not.

Merge matching ranks

Tap one plane, then tap another of the same rank. They fuse into the next rank, which earns far more than the two did apart.

Upgrade the loop

Loop speed multiplies what the whole fleet earns, so it pays off more the bigger your fleet gets.
